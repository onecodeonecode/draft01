The History of the National Hockey League

From a small league founded in Montreal to a global sport, the NHL's story is shaped by legendary players, unforgettable rivalries, expansion, and the pursuit of the Stanley Cup. Explore key eras and jump to trusted hockey resources below.

1917 · The beginning

A new league is born

The National Hockey League was founded on November 26, 1917, in Montreal, Quebec. The first season began with five clubs, setting the stage for a league that would grow for generations.

1920s–1930s · Early years

Challenges and growing popularity

The early decades brought financial challenges, changing lineups, and a growing fan base. The league's identity developed as players and clubs built its traditions.

1967 · Expansion

A bigger stage

The NHL doubled from six to twelve teams in 1967. The expansion transformed the league and introduced new rivalries and fan communities.

1942–1967 · Original Six era

Six teams, lasting legends

The Original Six era featured the Montreal Canadiens, Toronto Maple Leafs, Boston Bruins, Chicago Blackhawks, Detroit Red Wings, and New York Rangers.

Superstars change the game
