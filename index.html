CHEVELLE SS

AN AMERICAN ICON. A LEGEND ON WHEELS.

The Chevrolet Chevelle SS (Super Sport) became one of the most celebrated American muscle cars. With bold styling, powerful V8 options and a unmistakable road presence, it remains a favorite among classic-car fans.

Explore the Chevelle SS ›

Overview

The Chevelle was introduced for the 1964 model year. The SS package added performance-oriented styling and equipment, and the name became especially famous during the late-1960s and early-1970s muscle-car era.

Exact equipment and performance depend on model year, engine, transmission, and whether a car is factory-original or modified.

History Timeline

Milestones in the Chevelle story

  1. 1964 The Chevelle debuts as Chevrolet's mid-size car line.
  2. 1966 A redesigned body arrives; SS models become increasingly performance-focused.
  3. 1968 A new generation brings a shorter wheelbase and distinctive fastback-inspired styling.
  4. 1970 The SS 454 becomes a standout high-performance option.
  5. 1973+ Styling and regulations evolve; the classic muscle-car era changes.
