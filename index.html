    How to carve a pumpkin

    1. Choose a firm pumpkin with a flat base. Wash and dry it.
    2. Adults should cut a lid or opening with a sturdy pumpkin-carving tool, cutting away from hands.
    3. Scoop out seeds and strings with a large spoon. Save seeds for roasting if desired.
    4. Draw your face with a washable marker. Start with simple shapes and leave strong bridges between cutouts.
    5. Carve slowly with small controlled strokes. Children can draw designs and scoop with adult supervision, but adults should do the cutting.
    6. Place a battery-operated LED light inside, then display on a stable surface.

    Never let children handle carving knives. Keep real candles away from costumes, leaves and other flammable materials.
