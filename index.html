UNDERSTANDING ADHD

Signs of ADHD

Attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder can affect attention, activity level and impulse control.

🎯

Difficulty Paying Attention

Staying focused can be challenging, especially during tasks that feel repetitive or uninteresting.

Easily distracted Forgetful Loses things
⚡

Hyperactivity

Some people experience a strong need for movement or difficulty remaining still.

Restlessness Fidgeting Always moving
💥

Impulsivity

Acting or speaking quickly without always having time to consider the consequences.

Interrupting Impatient Acts quickly
📋

Organization Challenges

Managing time, tasks and everyday responsibilities may require extra effort.

Time management Procrastination Missed deadlines
🧠

Other Possible Signs

ADHD can look different from person to person and may affect everyday life in many ways.

Forgetfulness Task switching Difficulty starting tasks
Important: Having some of these signs does not necessarily mean someone has ADHD. A qualified healthcare professional can provide an appropriate assessment and diagnosis.
