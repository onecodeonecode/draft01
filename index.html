A little food for thought

Good things come in fruity packages.

Whole fruits provide water, fibre, vitamins, minerals and a variety of plant compounds. Different fruits offer different combinations, so enjoying a range of colours is a practical way to mix up your choices.

Nutrition note: figures shown on fruit cards are approximate values per 100 g of raw edible fruit. Variety, ripeness and preparation can change the numbers.
