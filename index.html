🔎 Common Signs

TAP TO EXPAND
🎯

Inattention

Difficulty maintaining focus or following details.

Examples can include being easily distracted, losing things, forgetting tasks, struggling to organize activities, or having difficulty finishing things that have been started.
⚡

Hyperactivity

Restlessness or difficulty staying still.

Someone may fidget, feel internally restless, talk frequently, have difficulty sitting for long periods, or feel driven to stay active.
💭

Impulsivity

Acting or responding before fully thinking things through.

Signs may include interrupting conversations, difficulty waiting, making quick decisions, or responding before a question is finished.
📋

Organization

Challenges with planning, priorities and time.

A person may underestimate how long tasks take, miss deadlines, forget appointments, or have difficulty managing multiple tasks.
⏰

Time Management

Difficulty estimating and tracking time.

Time blindness can make it harder to judge how long something will take or notice how much time has passed.
🌪️

Emotional Regulation

Strong or rapidly changing emotional responses.

Some people with ADHD experience frustration, impatience, emotional intensity or difficulty shifting attention after becoming upset.

🧠 ADHD & Diagnosis

ADHD is a neurodevelopmental condition. A diagnosis is based on a pattern of symptoms, their impact on everyday life, and information gathered through a professional assessment.

Having one or more of these signs does not automatically mean someone has ADHD.

If symptoms are interfering with school, work, relationships or daily activities, consider speaking with a qualified healthcare professional.
