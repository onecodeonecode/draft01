How to Quick-Chill Your Beer

1. Wrap your beer in a damp paper towel (optional but recommended)

2. Place the beer in the freezer

3. Start the 20-minute timer immediately

4. Remove when the timer goes off

Important Notes:

- Never leave beer in the freezer without a timer.

- Different beer sizes may require different times.

- Check occasionally if possible.
